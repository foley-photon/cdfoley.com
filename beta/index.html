Beta Simulators & Tools

Larger simulators and data tools that start here while they are in review. Each one says how it is tested, with a built-in self-test against published reference values or documented checks of its data, so you can see that before you rely on it. Beta tools whose data and checks are documented on the page are listed in the main tools hub and open to search engines; the others stay out of search results until that is done.

Beta, in review. Results are for exploration. Check numbers that matter against an independent calculation or measurement, and tell me about anything that looks wrong.

Charged-particle optics

Ion Optics Workbench

Build electrode geometries, solve the electrostatic potential, and fly ions and electrons through lenses, mirrors, analyzers, and RF devices.

 Spectroscopy and photochemistry

Absorption Cross-Section Atlas

UV, visible and VUV cross sections of about 1000 molecules from five open sources, overlaid and credited to the original papers, with values at laser lines, temperature interpolation, Beer–Lambert absorption and photolysis per pulse.

 Thermochemistry and photochemistry

Thermochemistry Lab

Built on Argonne's Active Thermochemical Tables: bond energies and photolysis thresholds with excited products, photofragment recoil and imaging radii, beam collision channels, and uncertainty budgets from ATcT's covariances.

 High vacuum

Conductance Lab

Conductance of tubes, slits, ducts, and whole pumping lines from molecular to viscous flow, and Monte Carlo conductance of CAD models.

 Windows and coatings

Glazing Performance (Open Data)

The glazing calculator with measured glass optical constants, thin-film low-e coatings calibrated to published datasheets, and nine measured IGDB products, side by side with the current model.
