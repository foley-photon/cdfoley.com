Glazing data redistributed with cdfoley.com
===========================================

This folder holds files copied byte for byte from two open-source projects of
Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL). Nothing in them has been changed.
The cdfoley.com glazing calculator reads them in the visitor's browser,
resamples the spectra to its own 5 nm wavelength grid for each calculation,
and shows only computed results. SHA-256 hashes of every file are listed in
../manifest.json.
