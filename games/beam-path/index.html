Beam Path Laser Puzzle

How to play. Tap an empty square to place the selected piece, tap it again to turn it, and once more to pick it up. Long-press or right-click a piece to pick it up at once. Pieces on a gray base are fixed; a curved arrow means it turns but can’t move. Stuck? Hint marks one square: where a piece goes, a piece to turn, or one that is in the way. A star in the level list means you used no more pieces than par. Your progress is saved in this browser. The square button next to Levels shows the game full screen.

Keyboard. The arrow keys move between squares, Enter or Space acts like a tap, R turns, Delete picks up, 1–5 choose a piece, H shows a hint, and U or Ctrl+Z undoes.

Reading the table. In the polarization levels, ticks across the beam mean it is polarized in the plane of the table (p) and dots mean perpendicular to it (s). In the two-color levels, the red laser is 633 nm and the blue one 450 nm; a detector with a colored ring and a letter sits behind a filter and counts only that color. Where beams overlap, their powers simply add: beams of different colors never interfere, and the game ignores interference between beams of the same color.
