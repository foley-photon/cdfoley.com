Science Hangman

Guess the science or engineering word one letter at a time. Six wrong guesses and the round is lost. Every word comes with a one-line definition, so you learn something either way.

Streak0Best streak0Wrong0/6

How to play. Type letters on your keyboard or tap them above. Stuck? Clue shows the word’s definition and Reveal letter fills in one letter; each costs one wrong guess, and neither can use up your last one. Press Enter for the next word. Pick a category and a level (Easy words are everyday science; Hard ones are specialist terms). Words you have not played yet come first. Your streak counts words solved in a row; skipping a word after you start guessing ends it.
