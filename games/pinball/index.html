How to play

Flippers: Z, ←, or Left Shift for the left; /, →, or Right Shift for the right. On a touch screen, tap the left or right half of the table, or use the buttons. Launch: hold Space or ↓ (or the Launch button) to pull the plunger back and release to fire; the longer you hold, the harder the shot. P pauses.

Shield: for the first 10 seconds after a launch, a drained ball is sent back. Top lanes: roll through all three to raise the end-of-ball bonus multiplier (up to 5×); the flippers shift which lanes are lit. Wormhole: the hole on the right captures the ball and fires it back toward the bumpers. Reaching Jupiter and Pluto starts a two-ball multiball.
