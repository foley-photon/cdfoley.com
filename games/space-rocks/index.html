Space Rocks

Pilot your ship through a field of drifting rocks. Every hit splits a rock into smaller, faster pieces. Clear the field to reach the next wave, and watch out for the saucer that shoots back.

Space Rocks

← → turn, ↑ thrust, Space fire, Shift hyperspace.

How to play: shoot every rock to clear the wave; large rocks split into two medium ones, and medium into two small. The bar along the top of the playfield fills as you clear the field, and each wave brings one more rock (up to 11), moving a little faster. From wave 2 a saucer flies through and shoots back. You start with three ships.

Controls: ← → or A D turn, ↑ or W thrust, Space fires (hold for rapid fire), Shift or H jumps to hyperspace, and P pauses. Hyperspace drops you at the clearest spot it can find and then needs 4 seconds to recharge; the meter at the bottom left of the playfield (and the Hyper button on a touch screen) fills as it charges. On a touch screen, hold the turn pad on the left (slide your thumb across it to change direction) and use Thrust, Fire, and Hyper on the right; tapping the playfield also fires.

Scoring: large rocks are worth 20, medium 50, and small 100; the saucer is worth 200, or 1,000 for the small one. You earn an extra ship every 10,000 points.
