Space Rocks

Pilot your ship through a field of drifting rocks. Every hit splits a rock into smaller, faster pieces. Clear the field to reach the next wave, and watch out for the saucer that shoots back.

Space Rocks

← → turn, ↑ thrust, Space fire, Shift hyperspace.

← → or A D turn, ↑ or W thrust, Space fires (hold for rapid fire), Shift or H jumps to hyperspace, and P pauses. Large rocks are worth 20, medium 50, and small 100; the saucer is worth 200, or 1,000 for the small one. You earn an extra ship every 10,000 points.
