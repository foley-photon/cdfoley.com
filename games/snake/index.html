Snake Game

Steer the beam to collect photons. Each one makes it longer and a little faster. Don’t run into yourself.

Snake

Steer with the arrow keys or WASD, swipe the board, or use the arrow pad. The beam waits for your first move.

How to play. Each photon you collect adds a segment and speeds the beam up a little. Hitting a wall, an obstacle, or your own tail ends the game; fill the whole board to win. After you press Start, the beam waits until you choose a direction, so you can get ready.

Controls. ← ↑ → ↓ or WASD steer; on a touch screen, swipe the board or use the arrow pad. Space or P pauses and resumes (so does steering), and the game pauses itself when you switch tabs. Enter starts a new game once one has ended.

Options. Relaxed starts slow and speeds up gently; Fast is for experts. With Wrap edges on, the beam passes through one wall and comes back through the opposite one. Obstacles scatters blocks across the board, always leaving every open square reachable. Options can be changed between games, and your best score is kept separately for each combination.
