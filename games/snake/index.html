Snake Game

Steer the beam to collect photons. Each one makes it longer and a little faster. Don’t run into yourself.

Snake

Steer with the arrow keys or WASD, swipe the board, or use the arrow pad.

How to play. Each photon you collect adds a segment and speeds the beam up a little. Hitting a wall, an obstacle, or your own tail ends the game; fill the whole board to win. After you press Start, the beam waits until you choose a direction, so you can get ready.

Controls. ← ↑ → ↓ or WASD steer; on a touch screen, swipe the board or use the arrow pad. Space, P, or Esc pauses (steering or Space resumes), and the game pauses itself when you switch tabs. Enter starts a new game once one has ended. New game during a game takes you back to the start screen and its options. The square button next to New game shows the game full screen.

Options. Pick them on the start screen before each game. Relaxed starts slow and speeds up gently; Fast is for experts. With Wrap edges, the beam passes through one wall and comes back through the opposite one. Obstacles scatters blocks across the board, always leaving every open square reachable. Your best score is kept separately for each combination.

Daily challenge. One board a day, the same for everyone: normal speed, 20 × 20 with walls, and obstacles, with the same photons in the same order for as long as your path doesn’t cover the next spot. Play it as often as you like; your best score of the day counts, and the streak counts the days in a row you played it.
