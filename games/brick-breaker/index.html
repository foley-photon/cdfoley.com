Brick Breaker Game

Bounce the photon off your mirror paddle to clear the wall. Each row is a color of the spectrum, and violet, the most energetic, is worth the most. Catch the power-ups that fall from broken bricks.

Brick Breaker

Move with the mouse, a finger, or ← →. Click, tap, or press Space to launch.

How to play: keep the photon in play and break every brick to clear the level. You have three lives (five on Easy); a photon that falls past the mirror costs one. Clearing a level earns a bonus of 500 points times the level number.

Controls: move the paddle with the mouse, by dragging on the playfield, or with ← → / A D. Click, tap, or press Space to launch; P or Esc pauses. New game during a game takes you back to the start screen, where you can change the difficulty. Where the photon hits the paddle sets its angle. Bricks with a white outline take two hits. The square button next to New game shows the game full screen.

Power-ups: W widens the mirror, M splits every photon into three, S slows the photons, G turns them into gamma rays for 6 seconds, so they punch straight through bricks, and + adds a life. After 15 layouts the cycle repeats, faster. Once you reach a level, you can start a later game there from the start screen.

Difficulty: Easy gives a wider mirror, slower photons, five lives, and more power-ups. Hard has a narrower mirror, faster photons, and fewer power-ups. Your best score is kept for each difficulty.
