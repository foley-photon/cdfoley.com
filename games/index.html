Games

Our own takes on ten classics, for a break between calculations. They're free, with no ads and no sign-up, and they work with a keyboard, mouse, or touch screen. Best scores are saved in your browser.

Arcade

Orbit Pinball

A 3D space pinball table with bumpers, a wormhole, multiball, and missions on a grand tour of the planets.

Brick Breaker

Bounce a photon off your mirror paddle to clear rainbow walls of bricks. Five layouts and four power-ups.

Space Rocks

Blast drifting rocks into smaller pieces, dodge the saucer, and survive wave after wave. A vector-style arcade shooter.

Paddle Ball

The classic two-paddle arcade duel, against the computer at three levels or with a friend. First to 11 wins.

Snake

Steer a laser beam to collect photons. It grows and speeds up with every catch. Optional wrap-around edges.

Puzzles

2048

Slide the tiles and merge matching numbers to build a 2048 tile. Swipe or arrow keys, with undo and saved progress.

Sudoku

Fresh puzzles with exactly one solution, from easy to expert, with pencil notes, hints, and mistake highlighting.

Minesweeper

Clear the board without hitting a mine. Three sizes, a safe first click, flags, chording, and best times.

Cards & words

Solitaire

Klondike, draw one or three. Drag or tap to move, unlimited undo, hints, and auto-finish.

Hangman

Guess science and engineering words letter by letter, and learn a definition with each one. Six categories.
