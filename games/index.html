Free Online Games

Thirteen games for a break between calculations: my own takes on eleven classics, plus a laser optics puzzle and a periodic table quiz. They're free, with no ads and no sign-up, and they work with a keyboard, mouse, or touch screen. Best scores and progress are saved in your browser.

Arcade

Orbit Pinball

A 3D space pinball table with bumpers, a wormhole, multiball, and missions on a grand tour of the solar system, plus a practice mode.

Brick Breaker

Bounce a photon off your mirror paddle to clear rainbow walls of bricks. Ten layouts, four power-ups, and a level select.

Space Rocks

Blast drifting rocks into smaller pieces, dodge the saucer, and survive wave after wave. A vector-style arcade shooter.

Paddle Ball

The classic two-paddle arcade duel, against the computer at three levels or with a friend. Play to 5, 11, or 21.

Snake

Steer a laser beam to collect photons. Pick the speed and board size, walls or wrap, and an obstacle mode.

Puzzles

Block Drop

Turn and drop falling blocks to clear lines as the speed climbs. Hold, next-piece preview, ghost piece, and touch controls.

2048

Slide the tiles and merge matching numbers to build a 2048 tile. Three board sizes, swipe or arrow keys, undo, and saved progress.

Sudoku

Fresh puzzles with exactly one solution, easy to expert, plus a daily puzzle, pencil notes, and hints that explain.

Minesweeper

Clear the board without hitting a mine. Three sizes or a custom board, a safe first click, optional no-guess boards, chording, and statistics.

Beam Path

Steer a laser to every detector with mirrors, beam splitters, and wave plates. 36 levels, up to polarization optics.

Cards, words & quizzes

Solitaire

Klondike, draw one or three, with standard or Vegas scoring, optional winnable deals, hints that explain, unlimited undo, and statistics.

Science Hangman

Guess science and engineering words letter by letter, and learn a definition with each one. Nine categories, three levels.

Periodic Table Quiz

Find elements on the table, match names and symbols, or race a 60-second sprint. Three levels, up to all 118.
