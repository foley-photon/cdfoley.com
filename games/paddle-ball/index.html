Paddle Ball

The classic two-paddle arcade duel. Return the ball past your opponent; where it hits your paddle sets the angle, and every rally speeds it up. Play to 5, 11, or 21 points, and win by two.

Paddle Ball

Move your paddle with the mouse, a finger, or W S / ↑ ↓.

One player: you are the cyan paddle on the left (at the bottom on a phone held upright). Move it with the mouse, by dragging on the court, or with W S or ↑ ↓. Two players: left uses W S, right uses ↑ ↓; on a touch screen, each player drags on their own half of the court. P pauses, M turns the sound on or off, and the square button next to New game shows the court full screen.

Serving and scoring: the first serve of a match goes to a random side. After every point the ball is served from the center toward the player who lost the point, and an arrow shows which way it will go. You score when the ball gets past your opponent’s paddle. A match is played to 5, 11, or 21 points, but the winner must lead by two: at 10–10 in a match to 11, play goes on until one side is two points ahead.

Wall practice: play alone against the far wall. The ball speeds up with every return, and three misses end the session. Records: your wins, losses, and win streak against the computer are kept for each level (a match you leave once a point is scored counts as a loss), along with your longest rally and your best wall rally. New game opens the setup, where you choose the mode, the computer’s level, and the match length.
