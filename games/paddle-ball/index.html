Paddle Ball

The classic two-paddle arcade duel. Return the ball past your opponent; where it hits your paddle sets the angle, and every rally speeds it up. Play to 5, 11, or 21 points, and win by two.

Paddle Ball

Move your paddle with the mouse, a finger, or W S / ↑ ↓. Two players: left W S, right ↑ ↓.

One player: you are the cyan paddle on the left. Move it with the mouse, by dragging on the court, or with W S or ↑ ↓. Two players: left uses W S, right uses ↑ ↓; on a touch screen, each player drags on their own half of the court (a phone held sideways gives each of you more room). P pauses.

Serving and scoring: the first serve of a match goes to a random side. After every point the ball is served from the center toward the player who lost the point, and an arrow shows which way it will go. You score when the ball gets past your opponent’s paddle. A match is played to 5, 11, or 21 points, but the winner must lead by two: at 10–10 in a match to 11, play goes on until one side is two points ahead.
