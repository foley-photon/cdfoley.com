Klondike Solitaire

Klondike: build each suit up from ace to king on the four foundations. In the tableau, stack cards down in alternating colors; only kings go in empty columns.

You won!

Drag cards to move them, or tap a card to send it to the best legal spot (foundations first). Tap the deck to draw; tap the empty deck to turn the waste pile over. Face-down cards flip automatically when uncovered. To take a card back off a foundation, drag it. When every card is face up, Finish takes the place of Hint and plays the rest up for you.

Daily deal: one deal a day for each draw mode, the same for everyone on that date, and checked to be winnable even with Vegas pass limits. Win one on consecutive days to build a streak. Once you have won the day’s deal, replaying it is practice and changes no statistics. Hint shows the most useful move and why; when nothing helps, it offers to restart the deal or deal a new one.

Keyboard: Tab to the table, then the arrow keys choose a pile or card (↑ in a column reaches a run). Enter or Space draws or sends the card to its best spot, 1–7 move it to that column, F to a foundation. D draws, H gives a hint, and U or Ctrl+Z undoes.

Settings (the slider button) hold the scoring, winnable deals, sending safe cards to the foundations by themselves (an ace or two, or a card that both piles of the other color are ready for), the sound, and Restart this deal, which deals the same cards again (Undo brings you back). The square button beside it shows the game full screen.

Standard scoring: 5 points for a card from the waste to the tableau, 10 for a card to a foundation, 5 for each card turned over, −15 for a card taken back off a foundation, and −100 (Draw 1) or −20 (Draw 3) each time you turn the waste over; the score never drops below 0. Vegas scoring: each game costs $52 and every card on a foundation pays $5; you get one pass through the deck in Draw 1 and three in Draw 3. A new scoring choice starts with your next game.

Winnable deals: before a deal reaches you, the computer plays it through with the face-down cards in view, under your draw and scoring rules, and deals it only if it wins. A win is always possible, but you can still get stuck, because you can’t see the face-down cards; Undo is unlimited. Your game in progress is saved in this browser.
