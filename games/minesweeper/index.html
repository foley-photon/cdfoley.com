Minesweeper

Clear every square that doesn’t hide a mine. Each number counts the mines touching that square. Your first click is always safe, and it always opens an area.

Mines10Time0Best—
5–40 wide, 5–30 high.

Click or tap a square to reveal it. Right-click or long-press to flag a mine; with Flag mode on, a tap flags and a long-press reveals. When a number has all its mines flagged, click it to open its other neighbors at once (chording); a middle-click or both mouse buttons also chord. Keyboard: Tab to the board, arrow keys move, Enter or Space reveals, F flags, C chords. The face starts a new game.

No-guess boards can always be cleared by logic from your first click, with no 50/50 guesses. Question marks adds a “?” step after the flag, for squares you’re unsure of. The timer pauses while this tab is hidden.
