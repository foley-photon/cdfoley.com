Minesweeper

Clear every square that doesn’t hide a mine. Each number counts the mines touching that square. Your first click is always safe, and it always opens an area.

Mines10Time0Best—
5–40 wide, 5–30 high.

Paused

The clock is stopped and the board is hidden.

Click or tap a square to reveal it. Right-click or long-press to flag a mine; with Flag mode on, a tap flags and a long-press reveals. When a number has all its mines flagged, click it to open its other neighbors at once (chording); a middle-click or both mouse buttons also chord. Keyboard: Tab to the board, arrow keys move, Enter or Space reveals, F flags, C chords, H shows a hint, P pauses, and N starts a new game. The face also starts a new game, and the square button shows the game full screen.

Boards. Beginner has 10 mines, Intermediate 40, and Expert 99. The daily board is a 16 × 16 board with 40 mines, the same for everyone that day. It starts with an area already open, it never needs a guess, and your streak counts the days in a row you clear it. On a tall, narrow screen a wide board stands on its side, and on a small touch screen a big board scrolls inside its frame.

Hints. Hint marks a square that must be safe, worked out only from the numbers showing and the mine count. Each hint adds 10 seconds to your time. If nothing is certain, it says so, and the board needs a guess there (a no-guess board never does). The clock stops while the game is paused or this tab is hidden.

Settings (the slider button): No-guess boards can always be cleared by logic from your first click. Question marks adds a “?” step after the flag, for squares you’re unsure of. You can turn off chording by a plain click, and turn the sound on or off.

Finished games on the three standard boards count, with no-guess boards kept separately. The daily board counts days: your first finished game on that day’s board is the day’s result (replays are practice), a day is won when that game clears the board, and its streak counts the days in a row. Abandoned and custom games aren’t counted. Statistics stay in this browser.
