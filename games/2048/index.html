2048 Puzzle Game

Slide all the tiles at once. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one with double the value. Build a 2048 tile, then keep going as far as you can.

Score0Best0Moves0

How to play: every move slides all the tiles as far as they go, then a new 2 or 4 appears in an empty square. Two tiles with the same number merge, and their sum is added to your score. Reach 2048 to win, then keep going for a bigger tile; the game ends when no move is left. A 5 × 5 or 6 × 6 board gives you more room, and each size keeps its own game, best score, and stats.

Controls: ← ↑ → ↓ or WASD slide the tiles; on a touch screen, swipe the board. U or Ctrl+Z undoes a move. With a screen reader, R reads out the board. Your game is saved in this browser, so you can come back to it.
