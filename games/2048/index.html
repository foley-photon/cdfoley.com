2048 Puzzle Game

Slide all the tiles at once. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one with double the value. Build a 2048 tile, then keep going as far as you can.

Score0Best0Moves0

How to play: every move slides all the tiles as far as they go, then a new 2 or 4 appears in an empty square. Two tiles with the same number merge, and their sum is added to your score. Reach 2048 to win, then keep going for a bigger tile; the game ends when no move is left.

Controls: ← ↑ → ↓ or WASD slide the tiles; on a touch screen, swipe the board. U or Ctrl+Z undoes a move, H shows a hint, N starts a new game, and M turns the sound on or off. With a screen reader, R reads out the board. The square button next to New game shows the game full screen. Your game is saved in this browser, so you can come back to it.

Hints and settings: Hint looks two moves ahead, averaging over where the next tile could appear, and points an arrow at the move it likes best. It favors open squares, rows and columns that rise toward one side, and tiles that can merge. A game in which you use a hint still counts as played, but its score and tiles don’t count toward your best or the tile statistics. The slider button opens the settings: a 5 × 5 or 6 × 6 board gives you more room (each size keeps its own game, best score, and statistics), tiles can show powers of two (2¹¹ for 2048, easier to read on a big board), and the sound can be turned off.
