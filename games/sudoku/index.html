Sudoku Puzzles

Fill the grid so that every row, every column, and every 3 × 3 box contains the digits 1 to 9 exactly once. Every puzzle is freshly generated, has exactly one solution, and can be solved by logic alone, without guessing.

How to play: click or tap a square, then a number. Show mistakes marks a digit that doesn’t match the solution (red, underlined) and counts it; Highlight conflicts rings two equal digits in the same row, column, or box. Notes pencil in candidates, and placing a digit clears it from the notes in its row, column, and box; Fill notes pencils in every digit that still fits. Hint fills one square and says why it must hold that digit. A best time counts only if you solve without hints.

Levels: Easy needs only singles (a digit with one possible square, or a square with one possible digit). Medium needs locked candidates as well (a digit confined to one line inside a box, or to one box along a line). Hard needs a naked or hidden pair or triple, and Expert an X-wing, swordfish, XY-wing, or XYZ-wing. The Daily puzzle is the same for everyone on the same date, one per level. Print gives a clean copy of the puzzle to solve on paper.

Keys: 1–9 enter a digit, 0, Backspace, or Delete erases, the arrow keys move, N switches to notes, P pauses, and Ctrl+Z undoes. Your puzzle is saved in this browser.
