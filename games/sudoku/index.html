Sudoku Puzzles

Fill the grid so that every row, every column, and every 3 × 3 box contains the digits 1 to 9 exactly once. Every puzzle is freshly generated, has exactly one solution, and can be solved by logic alone, without guessing.

How to play: click or tap a square, then a number. Notes pencils in candidates instead (or hold Shift while you type a digit), and placing a digit clears it from the notes in its row, column, and box; Fill notes pencils in every digit that still fits. Hint fills one square and says why it must hold that digit. A best time counts only if you solve without hints. The small number under each digit on the pad says how many are still to place.

Levels: Easy needs only singles (a digit with one possible square, or a square with one possible digit). Medium needs locked candidates as well (a digit confined to one line inside a box, or to one box along a line). Hard needs a naked or hidden pair or triple, and Expert an X-wing, swordfish, XY-wing, or XYZ-wing. The Daily puzzle is the same for everyone on the same date, one per level; a replay of one you already solved that day is practice, with no best time or statistics. Solve one on consecutive days to build a streak.

Settings (the slider button): Show mistakes marks a digit that doesn’t match the solution (red, underlined) and counts it; Highlight conflicts rings two equal digits in the same row, column, or box; you can also turn off the matching-digit highlight, hide the timer, or turn off the sound. The square button beside it shows the game full screen. Print this puzzle gives a clean copy to solve on paper.

Keys: 1–9 enter a digit (Shift for a note), 0, Backspace, or Delete erases, the arrow keys move, N switches to notes, H gives a hint, P pauses, and Ctrl+Z undoes. Your puzzle is saved in this browser.
