Laser, Optics, Vacuum & Quality Explainers

Practical write-ups on the physics and engineering behind laser processing, optics, vacuum systems, machine vision, spectroscopy, and production quality, by Casey D. Foley, PhD, a laser and high vacuum systems expert and physical chemist. They are written for the technicians, engineers, students, and managers who run or buy this equipment. Each article opens with a plain summary and a list of key terms, explains the ideas before the math, works through real numbers, states its assumptions, cites its sources, and links to calculators so you can run your own case.
